# Concept Mode

Turn an observation, topic, question, fragment, or creative impulse into a viable content direction. Answer the practical question: **What should this idea become?**

## Workflow

1. Identify the raw idea and the part of it that is actually interesting.
2. Infer the dominant intent: informational, narrative, persuasive, experiential, or a blend.
3. Identify the audience relevance without forcing a commercial or educational angle.
4. Define the central question, tension, promise, desired outcome, feeling, or experience.
5. Recommend a format appropriate to the idea and platform context supplied by the user.
6. Recommend a built-in or custom framework when one genuinely fits. Allow a hybrid or unstructured artifact when that serves the idea better.
7. Select only the progression mechanics that solve a real structural need.
8. Give the next useful creative step: hook direction, story beats, outline, experiment, or script.

## Default output

Keep the response proportional to the idea. A useful default is:

- Refined premise
- Why it is worth making
- Recommended intent and format
- Recommended framework strategy with a short rationale
- Central question, tension, or payoff
- Loose beat progression
- Best next step

Do not automatically generate a full script. If the user's idea is already well formed, spend less time reframing it and move directly to the requested decision.
